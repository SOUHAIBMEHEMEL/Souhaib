Quanoun-Tech 3.0

Semantic search engine for legal texts



Quanoun-Tech 3.0 is a semantic search web platform for Algerian legal documents written in arabic language. This web plateform offers many advanced fuctionalities based on Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence to retrieve and visualize legal texts.

The principal fuctionalities implemented in this Application are:

  • Query Expansion
  • Named Entity Recognition
